HOW BITEWISE MEASURES THIS

How the area and dish results are made

1. Menu similarity Compares menus in the same area. A higher score means the menus share more similar dish names.
2. Restaurant count Shows how many restaurants or food places are represented in the available area data.
3. Dish coverage Highlights dishes that appear on fewer menus. This is a research lead, not proof of demand or success.
