STEP 01 · COMPARE MENUS

Finding restaurants with similar menus

Compares dish names across menus in the same area. A higher similarity score means two menus share more similar dishes. It does not prove that the restaurants have the same owner.

STEP 02 · FIND LESS-COMMON DISHES

Finding dishes fewer restaurants offer

Counts how often dishes appear in local menus. A dish with low coverage is a research lead; the data does not prove that customers want it.

STEP 03 · TEST A MENU IDEA

Checking a proposed menu

Choose an area, enter one dish per line, and review how often those dishes already appear nearby. The system can also suggest spelling matches.

STEP 04 · REVIEW ESTIMATES

Separate order and revenue estimates

The analyst view compares estimated restaurant activity. These figures are benchmarks from limited data, not a promise of future orders or revenue.

HOW SIMILARITY IS MEASURED

Menu similarity score

Shared dish names and menu words → Similarity score (0% to 100%)

Results are estimates based on the available menu, area, and restaurant data. They are not guaranteed demand.
